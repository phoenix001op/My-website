Screen time

Turn on Screen time in 4 easy steps

Screen time needs a small free add-on for your laptop browser. It only saves website names on your own computer and does not slow anything down. Takes about a minute.

  1. Download Download tracker Then right-click the downloaded file and choose Extract files.
  2. Open your extensions page brave://extensions Open a new tab, paste it in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. Turn on Developer mode Flip the switch at the top right of that page.
  4. Click Load unpacked Choose the extracted studytimer-tracker folder. If it shows another studytimer-tracker folder inside, choose that inner one.

Done! Come back and refresh this page. It connects by itself and your screen time starts filling in.

Laptops only (Chrome, Brave or Edge). Phones cannot run it.
